## Description

**Choose an operative. Build a loadout. Survive the signal.**

RELAYWAKE is a free, 10-minute survival-action game that runs directly in your browser. Pick one of five operatives and one of two field themes, then combine active weapons and passive upgrades while the battlefield grows increasingly hostile.

### Features

- Fast runs designed to last about 10 minutes
- 5 operatives with distinct starting abilities and stats
- 23 active and passive upgrades
- 6 regular enemy families, a mid-run mini-boss, and a final boss
- Automatic attacks with keyboard, mouse, and mobile touch controls
- Local progression, settings, and run history
- Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, and French UI

### Controls

- **Move:** WASD or arrow keys
- **Choose an upgrade:** 1, 2, 3, or click/tap a card
- **Pause / resume:** P, Esc, or the HUD pause button
- **Attack:** Automatic
- **Mobile:** On-screen joystick and touch buttons
